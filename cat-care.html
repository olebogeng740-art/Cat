🐱 Caring for Cats

A Complete Guide to Keeping Your Feline Friend Happy and Healthy

🍽️ Nutrition & Feeding

Choosing the Right Food

  • Select high-quality cat food with adequate protein (25-30%)
  • Look for named meat sources as primary ingredients
  • Consider age-specific formulas (kittens, adults, seniors)
  • Both wet and dry food have benefits; many cats benefit from a mix

Feeding Schedule

  • Kittens: 3-4 meals per day
  • Adults: 1-2 meals per day
  • Senior cats: 2-3 smaller meals per day
💡 Tip: Always provide fresh, clean water. Many cats prefer running water, so consider a cat water fountain.

💊 Health & Veterinary Care

Regular Checkups

  • Schedule annual vet visits for adults, twice yearly for seniors (7+ years)
  • Keep vaccinations current
  • Discuss parasite prevention with your veterinarian

Dental Care

  • Brush teeth 3-4 times per week (daily is ideal)
  • Use cat-specific toothpaste
  • Watch for signs of dental disease: bad breath, difficulty eating, loose teeth

Common Health Issues

  • Urinary tract infections
  • Diabetes
  • Kidney disease
  • Obesity
  • Hyperthyroidism
⚠️ Warning: Seek immediate veterinary care if your cat shows signs of distress, stops eating, has difficulty urinating, or displays behavioral changes.

🏠 Environment & Enrichment

Creating a Safe Home

  • Ensure windows and balconies are secure to prevent falls
  • Keep toxic plants out of reach (lilies, sago palms, etc.)
  • Store medications and chemicals safely
  • Provide hiding spots and vertical spaces

Mental Stimulation

  • Interactive toys (feather wands, laser pointers)
  • Puzzle feeders to engage hunting instincts
  • Window perches for bird watching
  • Regular play sessions: 15-30 minutes daily
  • Cat tunnels and climbing trees
💡 Tip: Cats are crepuscular (most active at dawn and dusk). Schedule play sessions during these times for best results.

🧴 Grooming & Hygiene

Coat Care

  • Short-haired cats: Brush 2-3 times per week
  • Long-haired cats: Brush daily to prevent matting
  • Use appropriate brushes for your cat's coat type

Nail Care

  • Trim nails every 2-3 weeks
  • Provide scratching posts to maintain natural claw health
  • Use cat nail clippers or grinders

Ear & Eye Care

  • Check ears weekly for dirt or odor
  • Clean gently with a damp cloth if needed
  • Monitor eyes for discharge or cloudiness

Litter Box Maintenance

  • Scoop daily to keep the box fresh
  • Deep clean weekly
  • Provide 1 box per cat, plus 1 extra
  • Place boxes away from food and water

😺 Behavioral Health & Bonding

Understanding Cat Behavior

  • Tail position indicates mood (high = confident, low = afraid)
  • Purring can indicate contentment or stress
  • Slow blinks are a sign of affection
  • Head bunting shows territorial marking and affection

Bonding with Your Cat

  • Respect your cat's independence and boundaries
  • Use positive reinforcement and treats
  • Create a routine to build trust
  • Provide quiet spaces for relaxation
  • Use gentle petting and play to strengthen bonds

Handling Common Behavioral Issues

  • Scratching furniture: Provide scratching posts, use deterrents
  • Excessive meowing: Check for medical issues, ensure enrichment
  • Aggression: Avoid triggers, consult a behavioral specialist
  • Anxiety: Create safe spaces, consider calming products

👴 Senior Cat Care (7+ Years)

  • Increase vet visits to twice yearly
  • Provide orthopedic bedding for joint comfort
  • Keep litter boxes easily accessible (lower sides)
  • Maintain consistent routines
  • Monitor for signs of cognitive decline
  • Adjust nutrition for aging metabolism
  • Provide extra warmth and comfortable resting spots
